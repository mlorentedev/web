# Let's talk.

Whether you're a company, an individual or have a one-off need, write to me.

I'm actively taking on freelance work. We'll build it together if:

- your local architecture has outgrown itself and you need to migrate it;
- you need to decide which platform to build and in what order, and want a plan before you spend;
- you're not sure how to automate deployments and the changes your agents make;
- you run a Kubernetes or container cluster and don't quite know how to operate it;
- you only find out something is wrong when a customer tells you, and need metrics, alerts and SLOs that warn you first;
- you have a load, concurrency, integration or performance problem and don't know where to start.

I leave it working so you can run it without me.

Send me an email and within 24 hours I'll reply with a plan, a fixed quote and the next steps, or with what I still need to know to close it.
