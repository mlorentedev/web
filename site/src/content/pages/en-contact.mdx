# How to work with me

I design, build, and stabilize critical systems: from bare-metal hardware and Linux kernel internals up to Kubernetes platforms and sovereign AI execution harnesses for autonomous agents.

I do not sell fragmented consulting hours or draft PowerPoint decks to justify recurring calls. My focus is resolving structural architecture bottlenecks, delivering production-grade systems in reproducible IaC, and transferring ownership.

I operate primarily across two tracks:

### 1. Systems & Platform Implementation (Service-as-a-Software)
For organizations and engineering teams requiring sovereign infrastructure without cloud lock-in or unbounded API costs:
- **Bare-metal and hybrid Kubernetes platforms:** Automated deployment via Ansible and Terraform, GitOps with Argo CD, secure mesh networking (WireGuard), and zero-trust observability.
- **Local AI inference infrastructure:** Production deployment of inference runtimes (vLLM, Ollama) on dedicated hardware, coupled with deterministic multi-agent execution harnesses.
- **Deliverable:** Fully functional production infrastructure, 100% codified, audited, and with zero operational debt.

### 2. Strategic Systems Engineering (Staff / Technical Advisory)
For infrastructure product companies, agent runtime creators, and cloud-native platforms:
- Distributed systems architecture and core component engineering in Go.
- Deep integration with Kubernetes internals (`client-go`), custom controllers, and ephemeral process lifecycles.
- Design of deterministic runtime harnesses (process sandboxing, concurrency containment, and in-stream credential protection).
