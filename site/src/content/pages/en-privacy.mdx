# Privacy

*Last updated: 26 August 2026.*

You are here because the law requires it. It is short, because there is not much to say.

## Who is responsible

Manu Lorente, an individual, is the data controller for this site. Write to **manu@mlorente.dev** for anything on this page, including any of the rights listed at the bottom.

## What the site collects on its own

**Nothing.** There are no cookies, no analytics, no tracking pixels and no advertising. Nothing you do here is measured or profiled, and there is no consent banner because there is nothing to consent to.

That is a deliberate choice, not an oversight — Google Analytics was removed rather than kept behind a banner. Any future traffic measurement will be self-hosted and cookieless, and this page will say so before it is switched on.

## What the site stores in your browser

One entry, under `web:gh-metrics:v1`. It caches public GitHub statistics — repository and star counts — for six hours so the page does not re-fetch them on every visit. It holds no personal data and no identifier, it is never sent anywhere, and clearing your browser storage removes it.

## What you give me deliberately

### The newsletter

If you subscribe, the form sends your **email address**, the **name of the form** you used, its **traffic source**, and the fact that you **ticked the consent box**. Nothing else. There is no name field, and nothing is inferred about you.

- **Legal basis:** your consent, given by ticking that box. The form will not submit without it.
- **Processor:** [Beehiiv](https://www.beehiiv.com), which runs the newsletter and stores the subscriber list.
- **How long:** for as long as you are subscribed. Unsubscribe and the record goes with it. Every email carries an unsubscribe link, and it is one click.

### Email

If you write to me, I have your message and your address, held in the mailbox at Zoho. I keep correspondence as long as it is useful to have and delete it when it is not. It is never added to the newsletter list — subscribing is something you do, not something done to you.

## What the server records

Like any web server, this one writes a log line per request: **IP address**, timestamp, the page requested, and the browser's user-agent string. An IP address is personal data, so it is worth being explicit.

- **Why:** keeping the site up and keeping it from being attacked. CrowdSec reads these logs to block abusive traffic.
- **Legal basis:** legitimate interest in the security and availability of the service.
- **How long:** **a maximum of seven days**, after which logs are deleted automatically.

The site runs on my own infrastructure — a server at Hetzner in Germany — not on a third-party platform.

## Who else sees any of this

Only the two named above: **Beehiiv** for the newsletter and **Zoho** for email, each for the one job it does. Nothing is sold, rented, shared for advertising, or handed to anyone else. There is no advertising network anywhere on this site.

Both are processors outside the EU, and each publishes its own privacy terms and transfer safeguards.

## Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask for confirmation of whether I hold anything about you, a copy of it, correction of it, deletion of it, restriction of how it is used, a portable export, or you can object to a use of it. You can withdraw consent at any time, and doing so is as easy as giving it — for the newsletter, that is the unsubscribe link.

Ask at **manu@mlorente.dev** and I will answer. If you think I have handled your data badly, you can complain to your national data protection authority; in Spain that is the [AEPD](https://www.aepd.es).

## Changes

If what the site collects changes, this page changes first and the date at the top moves. If a change is significant — new processing, a new processor — subscribers will be told rather than left to notice.
