I'm Manu. I studied telecommunications engineering, specialised in electronics, and my first job, in 2015, was measuring image sensors on a test bench in Seville.

Eleven years later I'm still at the same company, Teledyne e2v, but the test bench kept growing. Camera firmware in Grenoble. Customers in Shenzhen, Spain and the US. And a Kubernetes platform that turned out an SDK per camera: onboarding a customer went from 120 days to 20–30.

Today the test bench is a cluster where my AI agents run. I never switched worlds. I just kept moving up a layer.
