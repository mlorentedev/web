I'm Manu. I studied telecommunications engineering, specialised in electronics, and my first job, in 2015, was on a test bench in Seville, validating silicon: new imaging chips, one at a time.

Eleven years later I'm still at the same company, Teledyne e2v, but the test bench kept growing. Digital logic in VHDL and firmware in C for cameras in Grenoble. Customers in Shenzhen, Spain and the US. And a Kubernetes platform that turned out an SDK per camera: onboarding a customer went from 120 days to 20–30.

Today the test bench is a cluster where my AI agents run. I never switched worlds. I just kept moving up a layer: from silicon to firmware, from firmware to the customer's system, and from there to the cloud.

Outside work I'm part of [Cloud Native Sevilla](https://community.cncf.io/cloud-native-sevilla/) and [NaN](https://nan.builders/), a community of people building with AI, and I give the odd talk when I can. And I [row](https://remoandaluz.es/).
