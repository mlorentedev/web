Alongside the job I've trained in application development, platforms and cloud, and I practise it on [KubeLab](/lab/), my homelab: mini PCs and Raspberry Pis at home, a Mac mini, two Jetsons and a couple of laptops with NVIDIA GPUs for local inference, and nodes on Hetzner, GCP and AWS. With AI agents I don't marry any vendor: I build [vendor-agnostic, idempotent harnesses](/ai/) to work in an automated, remote way.

And I like getting stuck into anything: giving a hand to people starting their careers, mentoring the juniors at my company, and taking part in communities like [NaN](https://nan.builders/), on AI and open models, [Cloud Native Sevilla](https://community.cncf.io/cloud-native-sevilla/) and the [AWS User Group Sevilla](https://www.meetup.com/aws-user-group-sevilla/). I give the odd talk when I can and, away from the screen, I [row](https://remoandaluz.es/).
